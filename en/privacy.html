Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

In short: I only collect the data you send me yourself, so I can reply to your inquiry. I never sell it and never send newsletters.

Who is responsible for your data

This website belongs to Oleksandr Panchuk, a web developer (“I”). For any questions about your data, email sanyok185@gmail.com or message @oleksandr_panchuk on Telegram.

What data I receive

  • Inquiry form data: your name, a contact (Telegram, WhatsApp or email), the type of website, budget and anything you write about your business.
  • Messages: if you write to me on Telegram or by email, I see your message and your public profile details.
  • Technical data: the website hosting may automatically log your IP address, browser type and time of visit.

Why I need it

To reply to your inquiry, discuss your project, prepare a proposal and, if we agree, do the work. I don’t use your data for advertising, don’t send newsletters and never sell it.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the checkbox in the form, and preparing and carrying out our agreement to work together.

Who your data is shared with

I don’t share your data with third parties, except for services the website and communication can’t work without: website hosting (Vercel, GitHub Pages), the service that delivers form inquiries, Telegram and email. They process data under their own privacy policies.

Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address.

How long I keep it

As long as needed to reply and work together, but no longer than three years after our last contact. I delete it sooner if you ask.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser only stores settings: your chosen theme (light or dark) and a note that the loading animation has already been shown. This stays on your device and is never sent to me.

Your rights

At any time you can ask what data I have about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent. Just email sanyok185@gmail.com and I’ll reply within 30 days.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
