Frequent questions

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of your business, examples you like, your logo and texts if you have them. We’ll sort out the rest in the brief.

Who writes the copy?

You can send your own. Or I help with the structure and a draft; we agree on it in the brief.

How much do the domain and hosting cost?

The domain is registered in your name and costs about $10 a year. Hosting for a coded site is often free, and Webflow starts at $14 a month. The site is fully yours, with no builder lock-in.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. With a CMS you add posts and products yourself, and I write a short guide.

How many edits are included?

14 to 30 days of free edits after launch, then a support package if you need one.

How does payment work, including from abroad?

In parts, 50/50. For clients abroad: Wise, PayPal or bank transfer.

Got a website idea? Let’s discuss it.

I’ll reply within 2 hours. The first consultation is free.
